## Design Methodology 2 ≥ 1 Collect & Analyse information 3 ≥ 1 Draw Conclusions 2 ≥ 1 RESEARCH FINDINGS SUMMARY Best Approach Found: Dual-MCU Architecture with Voltage Divider: Combines an Arduino UNO for precise real-time sensor polling and an ESP8266 (NodeMCU) for dedicated MQTT Wi-Fi communication, linked safely via a 220Ω/330Ω voltage divider to step down the 5V logic to 3.3V. Key Insight: Offloading Wi-Fi networking and MQTT blocking tasks to the ESP8266 prevents the Arduino from missing ultrasonic sensor echo pulses. Lowering the 5V TX signal to 3.3V RX using a resistor network completely eliminates the risk of burning the ESP8266 chip without needing a dedicated level shifter IC. Practical Recommendation: When designing IoT hardware combining different logic levels (5V and 3.3V), never connect TX/RX pins directly. Always isolate logic levels using a voltage divider. Most importantly, establish a Common Ground (GND) across all separate power domains on the breadboard to prevent serial data corruption and signal noise.
